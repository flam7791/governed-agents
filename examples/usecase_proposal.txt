Proposal from the Communications team (owner: comms.lead@aurora.example)

We would like an assistant that drafts first versions of press releases from our published
reports and internal briefing notes (internal information, nothing restricted). About 10 staff
would use it, around 300 drafts a month. Today a first draft takes about three hours; we expect
to save half of that. Drafts would always be edited and approved by a communications officer
before publication.
